Emergency Plumbing by N Maphikana Plumbing

Plumbing emergencies never happen at a convenient time. Whether it's 2am on a Sunday or a public holiday, N Maphikana Plumbing answers the phone and sends a plumber to stop the damage and fix the problem.

We handle every kind of urgent plumbing job across St Dumas, Kuils River, Kraaifontein and the Cape Town northern suburbs, and we always explain the cost before work begins.

What we do

  • Burst and leaking pipes isolated and repaired
  • Burst geysers made safe and replaced
  • Overflowing or blocked toilets
  • Sewer and drain backups
  • Taps and valves that won't close
  • Water shut-off and damage control

When to call an emergency plumber

  • Water coming through a ceiling or wall
  • No hot or cold water in the house
  • Sewage backing up or a strong drain smell
  • Your water meter keeps running with every tap closed
  • Water pouring from the geyser overflow pipe

Frequently asked questions

How quickly can you get to me?

We dispatch a plumber as soon as you call and give you an estimated arrival time on the phone. Call or WhatsApp 073 430 2133 any time, day or night.

What should I do while I wait?

Close your main water stopcock to stop the flow, and if the problem involves the geyser, switch the geyser off at the electrical distribution board.

Will I know the cost upfront?

Yes. We tell you the call-out fee before we dispatch and give you a price for the repair before any work starts.
