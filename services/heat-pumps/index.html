Heat Pumps by N Maphikana Plumbing

Water heating is one of the biggest costs on most electricity bills. A heat pump moves heat from the air into your water and can use around a third of the electricity of a standard geyser element.

We size the heat pump for your household, connect it to your existing geyser where possible and set it up for the best efficiency.

What we do

  • Household sizing and recommendations
  • Heat pump supply and installation
  • Retrofits to existing geysers
  • Timers and controls
  • Servicing and maintenance
  • Advice on combining with solar

A heat pump is a good fit if

  • Your electricity bill is high because of water heating
  • Your household uses a lot of hot water
  • You want hot water that's less affected by load-shedding schedules
  • Your geyser is due for replacement
  • Your roof isn't suitable for solar panels

Frequently asked questions

Do heat pumps work in winter?

Yes. Heat pumps are designed for the Cape climate; efficiency drops slightly in cold weather but they still heat water effectively.

Can a heat pump connect to my existing geyser?

In most homes, yes. We'll check your geyser's age and condition during the quote.

Are heat pumps noisy?

Modern units run quietly, similar to an air-conditioner. We'll recommend a good spot to install it.
