FAQ

Questions, answered.

Can't find what you need? Call or WhatsApp us any time.

Are you really open 24/7?

Yes. Our hours are 24 hours, 7 days a week, including weekends and public holidays. Call or WhatsApp 073 430 2133 and we'll send a plumber.

Will you help with my insurance claim for a burst geyser?

Yes. We provide the damage report, photos, quote and Certificate of Compliance your insurer needs, and can liaise with them directly.

Are your plumbers qualified and registered?

All our plumbers are qualified and registered with the Plumbing Industry Registration Board (PIRB), and every geyser installation comes with a COC.

Should I switch to a solar geyser or heat pump?

Both can significantly cut your water-heating costs. We'll assess your household size, roof orientation and budget to recommend the best option.

Which payment methods do you accept?

EFT, card (tap or swipe on site), and SnapScan. Financing is available on larger installations and renovations.
